# Roadmap

The aim is a simulator that reproduces HOI4 naval combat as closely as possible, verified against the game's own files, its executable, and recorded battles ([mechanics/README.md](mechanics/README.md)).

## Status (2026-10-20)

**Matches hoi4.exe 1.19.3** (decoded, engine rewritten to it): ship stats (S-STATS), hour loop and firing order, activation and cooldowns, targeting and screening, hit chance, damage, crits, retreat, submarines, positioning, experience and manpower (including the daily refill in combat), and air combat. Built-in defines and static modifiers are vanilla 1.19.3; a mod folder (`--defines`) overrides them.

**GUI** (2026-10-18): a local web app (`src/NavySimulator.Web`, [gui.md](gui.md)) edits scenarios, fleets and designs, steps battles hour by hour with rewind and per-ship combat logs, and runs batches. The engine's hour loop is now `BattleSession`, steppable, with a per-hour timeline and structured events.

**Ship designer** (2026-10-19): hulls, modules and ship types are imported from the game and mods (`NavySimulator import-ship-data`, or Settings → Import in the GUI) with the game's IDs and names; `data/` holds TFB's (dev checkout over vanilla 1.19.3). Designs fit one module per hull slot, and the designer rules are checked: slot categories, required slots, forbidden categories, count limits, and the ship type from hull and modules (S-DESIGN), which now gives the role and organization. A design can override the restrictions (`allowAnyModule`). The migrated designs keep their stats exactly and three of the four scenarios their results for every seed. `torpedo_cruiser_torpattack` had light cruiser armor, which TFB's torpedo cruiser hull does not take; it now has Heavy Cruiser Armour IV and stays a heavy cruiser, so `battle-scenario-bk` changed (20 seeds: 531 h instead of 497, 9.0 defender ships sunk instead of 9.6).

**Calibration** (2026-10-19): [`tools/ingame/`](../tools/ingame/README.md) replays a saved battle in the game for any number of dice seeds, unattended (about a minute each), and logs every combat hour and every shot from inside the game ([LOG finding](reverse-engineering/findings/LOG-ingame-logging.md)). First case, the TFB CL3 battle (`data/battle-scenario-la-vs-torp-cl3.json`, save `light_attack_vs_torp_cruiser_battle_start.hoi4`), 12 game seeds: 336 h, BUL 7075 HP left (sd 161), ROM 2802 (sd 72), 1.2 ROM ships sunk. The shot logs found two engine errors, now fixed: night was applied with weather off, and `naval_damage_factor` counted once instead of twice. The last difference was TFB's `smoke_generator` technology (`naval_retreat_speed = 0.2`), a country modifier; researches now carry country modifiers (S-STATS-COUNTRY), and with TFB's defines 200 simulator seeds give 338 h, 7056, 2801 and 0.9 sunk, with escapes taking as long as in the game for every decision hour. Second case, a mixed fleet (`data/jukki_usa_vs_bc_japan.json`: BB, SHBB and CL against BC, SHBB and DD), generated into a save by `tools/ingame/make_save.py`, one game seed: start values and every attack and piercing value matched, but capitals hit 1.13–1.7 times as often in the game. TFB's `screening_bonus` adds heavy and light gun hit chance factors (1 and 0.3 × screening) that the engine ignored; now every key of the screening bonuses applies (`MemberModifierRules`) and all simulated hit chances are within 1 % of the logged ones. With the scenario's experience (20) and weather off, 12 game seeds against 200 simulator seeds: 188.6 h against 189.9, USA (BUL) 25.2 ships sunk and 474 HP left against 25.5 and 474, losses per design within 0.3 ships; Japan kept 8421 HP against 8129: the USA side dealt 8 % less, traced to dice luck in the game runs (USA hits 5.5 per run below their logged odds; with that luck the simulator predicts the game within 1 standard error). Targeting weights, positioning (TFB's 60-ship fleet-size rule), the damage multiplier, organization damage and retreat decisions were all confirmed shot by shot.

**Replay** (2026-10-19): the logger records every roll of the combat update and every ship's start-of-hour state; `NavySimulator replay` fights each logged hour with the game's own dice from the game's state and compares every shot (S-REPLAY). Its first run found and fixed four rules: the target's `critical_receive_chance` (TFB damage control, crit chance × 0.8), the experience level taken at the start of the hour, the task-force manpower refill on every sinking (confirmed in the code: sink handler → `0xd77ff0` → `0xd7a7e0`), and organization regain of retreating and escaped ships (0.2 an hour). Now the engine makes exactly the game's rolls in all 174 hours and 10,249 of 10,257 shots match; the rest are damaged critical parts. Free-running against 18 game runs, the USA side still deals more in the simulator (3,768 against 3,384 HP); dice (≈ 170 HP), parts (≈ 85) and 1.4 standard errors of noise account for it.

**Critical parts** (2026-10-19): parts are imported from `common/units/critical_parts` (`data/critical-parts/`, with each module's and ship type's `critical_parts` and each slot's game order) and modelled (S-CRIT-PART, S-STATS-PARTS, S-STATS-COMBAT): the part chance, the merged candidates in the game's slot order, the weighted pick, the part's damage replacing the crit multiplier, the stat penalties, and the part modifiers (speed, maximum organization, organization regain, retreat chance) scaled by `naval_critical_effect_factor`. Manpower losses now truncate the HP ratio to fx5 as the game does. Target weights and picks are computed in the game's fixed point. 11 replayed battles (seeds 1 and 18–27, 120,090 shots) match every shot, each side's HP damage within 0.1 HP; only naval dominance's organization regain differs. Free-running, 200 seeds against the 27 distinct game runs: Japan dealt 11,720 against 11,726 HP, the USA 3,785 against 3,523; with the game runs' hit luck the simulator predicts 3,711, leaving 188 ± 77 HP (2.4 standard errors), which the dice (independent, uniform) and naval dominance (≤ 6 HP) do not explain: most likely chance. The CL3 battle still matches (339 h, 7,101 and 2,805 HP left against 336 h, 7,075 and 2,802).

**Doctrines and spirits** (2026-10-19, later): `NavySimulator import-doctrines` (and Settings → Import in the GUI) reads the naval and air doctrine folders (grand doctrines, tracks, subdoctrines with their rewards, milestones) and the navy and air spirits (with their `has_doctrine` locks) from the game and mods into `data/doctrines/` and `data/spirits/` (TFB dev checkout over vanilla: 3 naval grand doctrines, 26 naval subdoctrines, 54 spirits; the hand-written [LEGACY] spirits are gone). A scenario side sets per folder the grand doctrine and, per track, the subdoctrine and its mastery; the GUI's side editor has a doctrine editor (rewards picker, unlocked rewards, milestones) and spirits grouped by slot. Decoded from hoi4.exe ([S-DOCTRINE finding](reverse-engineering/findings/S-DOCTRINE-doctrines.md)): cumulative mastery and reward costs, milestones of complete tracks, where each kind of effect goes (country modifiers, sub-unit bonuses on the technology path, named equipment bonuses), `has_doctrine`. The combat getters were decoded in full at the same time ([S-STATS-COMBAT-GETTER](reverse-engineering/findings/S-STATS-design-stats.md#s-stats-combat-getter-the-attack-and-armor-getter-0xc34a50-fully-decoded), [modifier sources](reverse-engineering/findings/S-STATS-COUNTRY-modifier-sources.md)) and the engine now reads: the capital/screen/submarine attack and defence factors by sub-unit type (carriers none), `submarine_attack`, `naval_night_attack` × darkness, country `naval_damage_factor`/`naval_defense_factor`, ship AA modifiers, `navy_visibility`, `naval_torpedo_cooldown_factor`, `navy_casualty_on_hit`, positioning, screening efficiency and screening without screens, torpedo screen penetration and reveal chance, sortie and fighter sortie efficiency, carrier night traffic, and the naval strike and carrier air factors. Country-level `naval_enemy_fleet_size_ratio_penalty_factor` (Naval Tripwire), `naval_has_potf_in_combat_*` (Line of Battle) and `naval_enemy_retreat_chance` have no effect in battle: the game reads them from admirals only. Air doctrines are recorded and count for spirits, but are not simulated. The 11 roll-logged replays still match every shot (no baseline uses doctrines). With USA on Fleet in Being (Line of Battle and Integrated Operations complete) and Decisive Battle, the mixed-fleet case gives USA 5,899 HP dealt instead of 3,890 over 100 seeds: doctrines matter, and none of this is checked in game yet.

**Naval dominance** (2026-10-19, later): decoded a ship's naval dominance (hull `naval_dominance_factor` × speed, range, heavy attack and carrier size terms; S-DOMINANCE) and when a country controls a region; hulls now import `naval_dominance_factor` and `naval_range`. `rules.navalDominanceEnabled` gives the side with more dominance from its ships at the start the +10 % organization regain for the whole battle; it is on in the three in-game scenarios, and `make_save.py` writes the fleets' totals into the battle region. The formula reproduces the CL3 save's region values exactly, and a daily update model the hour each logged mixed-fleet battle hands dominance to Japan in 26 of 27 runs (the old saves started from the CL3 values). Replays: still every shot; organization differences 10,210 → 3,825, all after that hand-over.

**MIOs** (2026-10-19, later): `NavySimulator import-mios` (and Settings → Import in the GUI) reads the MIOs whose equipment types name ships, with `include` resolved (overridden fields, merged initial trait, `add_trait`/`remove_trait`/`override_trait`), the trait trees with their grid positions, parents, exclusions, equipment limits and bonuses, the MIO policies and the equipment groups, into `mios/` (one file per game file), `mio-policies/` and `equipment-groups/` (TFB dev checkout over vanilla: 123 MIOs, 1,794 traits, 11 policies, 5 groups; the two hand-written MIOs, used by no design, are gone). A design names its MIO; each scenario side unlocks traits and attaches a policy per MIO (`mios`), as the country does in the game, and the bonus goes through the decoded S-STATS-MIO sum (S-MIO). Equipment groups (`mio_cat_eq_all_battleship`, ...) now also count for doctrine and spirit equipment bonuses (none uses one yet). The GUI's side editor draws each MIO's tree on the game's grid (click to unlock, red for setups the game would not allow), with the policy; the design editor lists the MIOs that cover the hull. A side can name its country (which limits its MIOs to those the country can have, `countries/` imported with the MIOs) and assign an MIO, or none, to any of its designs (`designMios`); the side editor's doctrines, MIOs, researches and spirits are foldable sections. The old in-game check (39_battleship_balanced with the armor and light-attack branch of the battle-line tree: HP 540, armor 51, AA 23.4, cost 14,178) now runs on the imported tree and still matches. Decoded from hoi4.exe (two findings): the include resolution, the tree rules, the equipment matching (a design matches by its hull and the hull's default modules, not its installed modules; checked against the 173 MIO variants of a TFB 1936 save), the policy sum and the timing. The same matching now scopes doctrine, spirit and named equipment bonuses; no existing scenario changes (every ship's stats identical, replays exact). Not checked in a game battle yet.

**Admirals** (2026-10-19, later): `NavySimulator import-leaders` (and Settings → Import admirals) reads every character with a `navy_leader` block (with the country that recruits it), the admiral traits and the navy skill levels into `data/admirals/`, `data/leader-traits/` and `data/leader-skills/` (TFB dev checkout over vanilla: 202 admirals of 38 countries, 101 traits; TFB's BUL and ROM recruit the debug admirals). A scenario side has an `admiral` (replacing the unused `commander` string): picked from its country's admirals and edited, or made up; the GUI's side editor has an Admiral section (skills with what each level gives, traits grouped with their effects, the fleet's total, trait-tree warnings). The engine builds the game's two leader containers (S-ADMIRAL-MOD: skill levels and traits in A, traits only in B, efficiency from `skill_bonus_factor`) and reads them where the game does: every ship modifier read (with the traits' `sub_unit_modifiers` for the ship's sub-unit), positioning and screening, the enemy's fleet-size penalty factor and retreat chance, and the attack getter's night and sub-unit adjusters. Not simulated: experience and trait gain in battle, medals, naval headquarters, terrain adjusters, air sub-unit blocks, pride of the fleet. No baseline has an admiral, so the 11 replays still match every shot; nothing about admirals is checked in game yet (`make_save.py` does not write them).

**Pride of the fleet** (2026-10-19, later): a scenario side names one design of its fleet as its pride of the fleet (`prideOfTheFleet`; the first ship of that design; the GUI offers capital ships and carriers, the ships the game allows). Decoded ([S-POTF finding](reverse-engineering/findings/S-POTF-pride-of-the-fleet.md)) and simulated: the ship's own `pride_of_the_fleet` block (half the gun and torpedo crits, 1.5 times the air crits as the game does, +25 % experience gain), and while it is afloat (escaped included) the commanding admiral's `naval_has_potf_in_combat_*` for the whole side. With it came the experience gain modifiers (`experience_gain_navy_unit(_factor)`, the sub-unit combat factor; S-XP-GAIN) and the air crit receive factor for every target (country damage control now raises air crits, as in the game). `make_save.py` writes the pride into battle saves; the admiral is still not written. All 12 roll-logged replays still match every shot (no baseline has a pride of the fleet, admiral or carrier).

**Air verified in game** (2026-10-20, overnight): five carrier and land-based test scenarios (CV mirror, bombers against fighters and CAS, land bombers against land fighters, carriers with land planes on both sides, and the user's USA vs Japan scenario with Japan attacking as in game) were generated into saves (`make_save.py` now also writes land-based wings on their missions) and logged in about 110 game battles with an extended air logger (a sixth batch, the CV mirror with weather and night on, followed). The replay now covers air (target picks on the game's integer draws, wings, aces, disruption and counted planes from the log, strike-by-strike comparison). Fixed from the logs: the air target pick, fleet AA (escaped ships count), interception defenders, the side update order and a new side's screening, the game's counter-0 creation update, the stance window's hour counter, unclamped carrier sortie experience, carrier crew (deck-space manpower), air crit part damage, land bombers' doubled disruption. New: the region's air war between the battle's air wings (A-REGION: losses, disruption, escorts, the strike efficiency), aces (A-ACE, templates imported from `common/aces`). The free-running engine now matches the game within noise in both carrier scenarios, the land scenario and the user's scenario ([calibration.md](calibration.md#air-tests-2026-10-20)); before the region's air war it sank almost the whole defending fleet in half the game's time. Open: the region's air detection and the land wings' coverage (inputs for now), who gets a region mission (an input), the USA's air damage in the mixed carrier + land case (×1.3–1.5), accidents, weather type 2's terms (with weather on, replayed air damage +20 % and the game's gun hit chances 3–4 % above the engine's).

**Night, weather and naval terrain** (2026-10-20, day): decoded from hoi4.exe and checked on the 10 logged weather battles ([S-NIGHT](reverse-engineering/findings/S-NIGHT-darkness.md), [S-WEATHER terms](reverse-engineering/findings/S-WEATHER-weather-terms.md), [S-WEATHER-SIM](reverse-engineering/findings/S-WEATHER-region-simulation.md), [S-TERRAIN](reverse-engineering/findings/S-TERRAIN-naval-terrain.md)). `NavySimulator import-regions` (and Settings → Import sea regions) writes the sea regions (naval terrain, weather periods, province positions, map size) and naval terrains to `sea-regions/` and `naval-terrains/` (TFB: 106 regions, 4 terrains). A scenario has `regionID`, `provinceID` and `startDate`: the darkness follows the province's map position, the day of the year and the hour in the game's fx5 arithmetic (every logged darkness to five decimals, read one game hour late), with the old night window only as a fallback. `weather` is clear, one weather for the whole battle (with `arcticWater`), or `simulated` (the game's 45-hour region visits, period weights, temperature rule and durations, on its own random stream). The region's weather blocks apply whole, through each ship's `naval_weather_penalty_factor` term (TFB super-heavies take none), to hit chance, speed (so hit profile and escape), retreat speed and detection; whole to positioning and screening, carrier traffic and naval strike targeting. That explains the weather batch's offsets: rain slows the targets (+4.2 % hit chance) and cuts naval strikes by 20 %; the replay of seed 1 now matches 1,693 of 1,694 shots and every air strike. The naval terrain enters attack and armor (sub-unit `units` adjusters, the admiral's terrain traits), combat speed, visibility and positioning. 100 simulator seeds with the simulated weather give the game's means (calibration.md). Weather-off battles and replays are unchanged, byte for byte.

**Region air war decoded, air doctrines** (2026-10-20, afternoon): three findings ([A-DETECT](reverse-engineering/findings/A-DETECT-region-air-detection.md), [A-REGION-SYNC](reverse-engineering/findings/A-REGION-SYNC-carrier-wing-region.md), [A-STATS](reverse-engineering/findings/A-STATS-plane-stats-and-air-modifiers.md)). A side's air detection in the region (0xf77730: its own region missions' planes, radar coverage, territory, the region's darkness and weather; new input `radarCoverage`) now sets the land wings' weight, the disruption and the escorts; reading it found two engine errors (the disruption share took the defender's capacity after the attacker's decrement, so a lone land wing was never disrupted; the defender weight truncated to whole planes). Fresh wings carry the experience malus (TFB −0.15 air attack, defence, agility) and wings with a region mission their mission efficiency and home defence or power projection. Carrier wings get the region at their task force's naval mission tick out of combat, so `carrierWingsOnRegionMission` now defaults to the attacker yes, the defender no; their effectiveness is the supply input (the logged 0.734 was ROM's supply) × deck crowding × (1 + `air_mission_efficiency` of country, admiral and weather), and `make_save.py` can put the defender's wings on the region list. Air doctrines and spirits are simulated: plane sub-units carry their categories (re-imported), doctrine blocks raise plane stats as factors (trunc((1 + Σ) × stat)), and the air keys the naval battle reads are modelled (the rest are listed as without effect or ignored). Checked on the logged air battles: `air_land_vs_fleet` matches the game's disruption within 1 % in 596 of 600 air hours with no hand-set `disruptionFactor` (land fighters' `missionEfficiency` 0.46, the coverage of the region from Burgas being an input), its strikes' air damage exactly; the carrier scenarios' replays are unchanged or better; 100 simulator seeds per air scenario stay within noise of the game. The 10 logged MIO + admiral + doctrine battles (`..._mio_admiral_battle_start_20261020_002753`, logged overnight, never replayed before) replay every shot (75,463) with the setup without the user's later carriers. Checked in game the same afternoon (`air_cv_doctrines`, 10 roll-logged runs): air doctrines on both sides, the defender's carrier wings on the region list from the generated save, and a pride of the fleet each; 1,151 of 1,173 strikes and every shot replay exactly, the region disruption within 1 % in 9,314 of 9,548 wing hours (without the defender on the list: 7.7k instead of the game's 142k). The MIO + admiral batch is now the calibration case `jukki-usa-vs-bc-japan-mio-admiral` (setup `jukki_usa_vs_bc_japan_mio_admiral.json`, fleet `Jukki_bb_fleet_usa_no_cv`).

**Land wings from their air bases** (2026-10-20, evening): three more findings ([A-COVERAGE](reverse-engineering/findings/A-COVERAGE-land-mission-effectiveness.md), [A-MIXED](reverse-engineering/findings/A-MIXED-plane-variants-in-a-wing.md), [A-ACCIDENT](reverse-engineering/findings/A-ACCIDENT-land-wing-accidents.md)). A land wing names its air base (state); its effectiveness is the coverage of the battle region by its range from the state's centre (`import-regions` now reads `provinces.bmp`: the game's province positions and boxes, and `air-bases/`) × the base's crowding (level × AIRBASE_CAPACITY_MULT against every plane there) × supply (an input). The 0.6 at Burgas in the logged battles was crowding (the template's 1,000 BUL fighters at the level-10 base), so the land scenarios have no fitted inputs left. Wings hold their variants: the stock refills newest first, losses take the oldest, stats are the plane-share mix cached each hour (the land strikes' replays went from 2,239 to 2,655 of 3,335 matching in `air_cv_land_mix`, 422 to 581 of 620 in `air_land_vs_fleet`). Land wings have the hourly accident pass (about half a plane per 100-plane wing in a 300-hour battle; carrier wings none), on the engine's own rolls (the game's local generator is not logged). The surface replays still match every shot.

**One constant roll** (2026-10-26): `run_battles.py --constant-roll U` forces every combat roll the engine also makes to one value in the game, and `NavySimulator replay --constant-roll U` fights the logged battle free from the game's first hour with the same value (S-REPLAY-CONSTANT). The mixed-fleet save with U = 0.05 (`output/ingame/constant_roll/`, 137 h): the engine fights the identical battle, every one of 6,164 shots and both sides' damage to 0.1 HP, ending in the same hour. It found one drift the hourly replays cannot see, the escape progress's fx5 truncation (fixed; the 11 baseline replays still match every shot). A plain scenario run gives a different battle, because the game puts super-heavy battleships first in each side's member order; with random rolls the order does not matter, with a constant it decides every target pick.

**Constant-roll sweep** (2026-10-26, later): seven more runs (mixed fleets at U 0.02 and 0.12 and with weather and night on, CL3 at 0.05 and 0.10 (a 2,000-hour stalemate), the MIO + admiral save at 0.05 and 0.08). Two rules fixed: positioning's detection bonus now reads the effective surface detection with `naval_detection` (rain lowered it in game, S-POS-DETECT), and naval dominance follows the decoded daily update (S-DOMINANCE-DAILY; a side starts at `navalDominanceStart`, else its ships' sum). Now every run matches every shot and both sides' damage, ending in the game's hour; left are the known last-hour regain and experience, one manpower value off by one (fx5 damage) and a fleet AA rounding. The baseline replays lost their dominance-related organization differences (about 3,800 to about 220, all in the last hour).
